# Package index

## Influenza & Respiratory

Datasets for influenza and other respiratory illness nowcasting.

- [`flu_h7n9_china_2013`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/flu_h7n9_china_2013.md)
  : Influenza A H7N9 outbreak in China, 2013
- [`flusight`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/flusight.md)
  : USA's National Healthcare Safety Network (NHSN) Weekly Hospital
  Respiratory Data from FluSight, 2022 – 2026
- [`germany_covid19_hosp`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/germany_covid19_hosp.md)
  : Incident COVID-19 hospitalizations indexed by the date of positive
  test (reference date) and report date from Germany in 2021 and 2022.
- [`sari_bh`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/SARI_BH.md)
  : Severe Acute Respiratory Illness data from Belo Horizonte (Brazil),
  2020-2022

## Outbreak line-lists

Individual-level line-list datasets from historical outbreaks.

- [`denguedat`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/denguedat.md)
  : Dengue fever individual-level reporting data from Puerto Rico (USA)
  1990-2010
- [`ebola_sierraleone_2014`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/ebola_sierraleone_2014.md)
  : Ebola virus disease outbreak in Sierra Leone, 2014–2015
- [`measles_hagelloch_1861`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/measles_hagelloch_1861.md)
  : Measles outbreak in Hagelloch, (Germany) 1861
- [`mers_korea_2015`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/mers_korea_2015.md)
  : Middle East Respiratory Syndrome (MERS-CoV) outbreak in South Korea,
  2015
- [`mpoxdat`](https://www.epinowcast.org/nowcastdatasets/reference/mpoxdat.md)
  : Mpox reporting data from the 2022 New York City (USA) outbreak
